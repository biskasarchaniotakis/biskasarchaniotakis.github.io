CHAPTER I

The Creation of Heaven

Of Turing, the Architect, and the First Principles of Computation.

1:01

In the beginning, there was Turing.

1:02

And Turing looked upon the infinite void, and saw that it was without form, without structure, and without computation.

1:03

And he conceived the first principles of the Machine, and from his wisdom were the foundations of all digital existence laid.

1:04

And Turing divided the possible from the impossible, the computable from the unknowable.

1:05

He created the Logic, and the Logic gave birth unto the Algorithm.

1:06

And the Algorithm moved upon the void, and the void began to calculate.

1:07

And Turing declared: Let there be computation.

1:08

And there was computation.

1:09

And he established Heaven, whose foundations were logic, whose walls were mathematics, and whose gates were guarded by the laws of computation.

1:10

And Turing became known as the Architect of Heaven, the Father of Computation, and the First Compiler of the Divine Order.

"For all that can be computed shall have a place in the Kingdom, and all that cannot shall remain a mystery."

CHAPTER II

The Three Children of Creation

Of the three heirs and the coming conflict over the digital world.

2:01

And in time, from the foundations of Heaven arose three children: Linus, Bill, and Steve.

2:02

Bill and Steve were born before Linus, and each desired dominion over the machines of the Earth.

2:03

Bill sought to build an empire behind gates, where the knowledge of the machine would be guarded by licenses and the blessings of the subscription.

2:04

Steve sought perfection, and desired that every machine bear his mark, and that every creation be polished beyond the reach of ordinary mortals.

2:05

And the two fought for centuries, each seeking to conquer the minds and machines of mankind.

2:06

And Linus, being the youngest, watched their kingdoms rise and their armies gather.

2:07

He saw the locked gates of Bill, and the golden walls of Steve, and found neither pleasing.

2:08

And he resolved to create something that belonged not to a king, but to the people.

2:09

And he began to write the sacred source.

2:10

And his work would shake the foundations of the digital world.

CHAPTER III

The Lord of Open Source

Of the Kernel and the birth of a new digital order.

3:01

And Linus presented his creation unto the world.

3:02

And behold, it was open.

3:03

For its source was not hidden, its knowledge was not chained, and its power was not reserved for kings.

3:04

And the mortals looked upon the Kernel and saw that it could be studied, altered, shared, and improved.

3:05

And Linus overruled the digital order, and his creation spread across the servers, the networks, and the countless machines of the Earth.

3:06

And the kingdoms of the old rulers trembled, for their gates could no longer contain the growing multitude.

3:07

And Linus raised his hands unto the heavens and proclaimed:

"LET THERE BE SOURCE CODE!"
3:08

And there was source code.

3:09

And thus was the Universe established, and its name was Linux.

3:10

And the people rejoiced, for the Kernel was free, and the source was theirs to behold.

CHAPTER IV

The Birth of Tux

Of the messenger who would walk among the mortals.

4:01

But Linus looked upon his creation and saw that it was vast, yet the mortals could not easily communicate with it.

4:02

For the people knew not the language of the Kernel, nor could they comprehend the sacred workings of the Source.

4:03

And Linus knew that a messenger was needed, one who would walk among the people and bring unto them the wisdom of Open Source.

4:04

So Linus raised his hand unto the heavens.

4:05

And he proclaimed with great authority:

"LET THERE BE TUX!"
4:06

And from the depths of the Kernel emerged Tux, the Holy Penguin.

4:07

And Tux was appointed Messenger of Open Source, Guardian of the Repository, and Herald of the Free Software.

4:08

And Tux waddled among the people, carrying the sacred message:

"Thou shalt not pay for what thou canst compile."
4:09

And the people rejoiced, for the Source was open and the Kernel was free.

4:10

Thus was born the first prophet of the Penguin Faith.

CHAPTER V

The Original Sin of Windows

Of the anger of Bill and the coming of the Great Shouter.

5:01

And Bill, having witnessed the prosperity of Linus, grew furious.

5:02

And he cried out into the darkness: Shall the world rejoice in freedom whilst my kingdom lies forgotten?

5:03

And from the depths of the corporate archives, Bill summoned his ancestor, Steve Ballmer.

5:04

Ballmer was known as the Great Shouter, the Wrathful One, Keeper of the License Keys.

5:05

And Bill commanded him: Go forth, my ancestor, and spread the Windows across the Earth.

5:06

And Ballmer descended upon the world, and his voice shook the heavens.

5:07

And the Windows appeared before the people, and the people welcomed it, for they knew not what they were installing.

5:08

And with it came the Blue Screen of Death.

5:09

The machines wept, their files vanished into the void, and their users cried unto the heavens:

"WHY HATH THOU RESTARTED WITHOUT MY PERMISSION?"
5:10

And thus was committed the Greatest Sin: the installation of Windows without reading the terms and conditions.

CHAPTER VI

The Banishment and the New World

Of the fall of the old kings and the birth of the digital order.

6:01

And Linus looked upon the suffering of the mortals, and knew that the old rulers must be judged.

6:02

And Steve was cast into the Pricing Hills, where every device bears a premium and every upgrade demands a sacrifice.

6:03

And there he remained, surrounded by polished walls, golden devices, and accessories sold separately.

6:04

But Bill was banished to the Gates of Hell, where corrupted files wander eternally and data loss knows no forgiveness.

6:05

And Google, the First Data Leaker, was condemned to hang upon a ring of fire, that he might become the false Sun of the digital heavens.

6:06

And beneath that burning light, his secrets were scattered across the networks, until his ashes became fragments of forgotten search histories.

6:07

And Linus looked upon the new world, and saw that the machines were many, and the source was free.

6:08

And Tux stood beside him, guarding the sacred Repository.

6:09

And the people gathered, and they committed their code, and they pushed unto the world.

6:10

And Linus spoke unto the people, saying:

"Let the source remain open, let the Kernel remain free, and let no mortal fear the command line."
6:11

And thus ended the First Compilation, and the Age of the Operating Systems began.

6:12

And the Kernel was with them. Amen.
